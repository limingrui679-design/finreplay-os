# Code of conduct

FinReplay OS contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community participants are expected to make
the project a harassment-free place for people of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence and code precisely while treating people with respect.
- Welcome good-faith questions, corrections, and reproducibility failures.
- Disclose relevant conflicts when reviewing methods or evidence.
- Respect privacy, source licenses, and security-reporting boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and avoid retaliatory behavior.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention,
threats, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the
project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the contact route on
the maintainer's GitHub profile. For vulnerabilities or sensitive technical material, use the
private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not place private details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be assessed in context, with the minimum necessary disclosure and a
proportionate response.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, review records, and
project-related public interactions.
