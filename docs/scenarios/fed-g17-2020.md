# March 2020 Federal Reserve G.17 industrial-production boundary replay

This counted scenario places a historical decision boundary at 2020-03-18 12:00 UTC. Its two
inputs are the January and February total industrial-production monthly changes reported in
archived Board of Governors G.17 releases. The March change is locked separately as a
post-decision event and is absent from every ReplayPack source record.

The source is the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. This case does not claim that
FinReplay existed at the historical decision time, observe plant-level production or transactions,
identify a causal pandemic, policy, or industrial mechanism, or predict a future monthly change.

## Paired official releases and knowledge boundary

`scenarios/fed-g17-2020/input-lock.json` contains:

- February 14 release for January: total industrial production declined `0.3%`, or `-30` basis
  points; total index `109.2`; capacity utilization `76.8%`;
- March 17 release for February: total industrial production increased `0.6%`, or `60` basis
  points; total index `109.6`; capacity utilization `77.0%`.

For each release, the supporting adapter retrieves the official archived HTML and 19-page PDF. It
requires both forms to match on release identity, reference month, headline change, total index,
capacity utilization, manufacturing, mining, utilities, year-over-year change, and the prior-month
revision bridge. Each PDF must state exactly one 9:15 a.m. EST/EDT release time and have 19
nonblank US Letter pages.

The January release states 9:15 a.m. EST, making its exact availability time 2020-02-14 14:15 UTC.
The February release states 9:15 a.m. EDT, making its exact availability time 2020-03-17 13:15 UTC.
Both precede the decision boundary. The timezone conversion is checked under `America/New_York`;
the repository does not infer historical publication from a current server header.

ShockCompiler uses only the two decision-time release snapshots:

- latest-known persistence baseline: `60` basis points;
- one known January-to-February increase: `90` basis points;
- stress endpoints: persistence at `60`, or one repeat of the known increase at `150`;
- range width: `90` basis points;
- probability assigned: none.

The range is not a forecast, confidence interval, calibrated coverage statement, plant model,
macroeconomic causal model, trading signal, or policy recommendation.

## Disjoint post-decision breach and revision

`scenarios/fed-g17-2020/event-lock.json` contains the April 15 release for March: total industrial
production declined `5.4%`, or `-540` basis points; total index `103.7`; capacity utilization
`72.7%`. Its paired HTML and 19-page PDF state a 9:15 a.m. EDT release time, making the fact
available at 2020-04-15 13:15 UTC, after the decision boundary.

The event release also revises February from the earlier `0.6%` snapshot to `0.5%`, a change of
`-10` basis points. The event record retains both values; the input lock remains `60` basis points
and is never overwritten. The reported March change is `600` basis points below the previously
declared lower endpoint of `60`. The verifier requires that miss to remain visible. It does not
widen the range, relabel the outcome as a success, or leak the outcome or revision into the pack.
A transparent miss is valid reproducibility evidence but strongly negative evidence for coverage
or forecast claims.

## Four relevant engines

TimeVault reconstructs the two-release decision set; ShockCompiler compiles the no-probability
persistence-or-one-known-increase endpoints; TrialCourt retains and rejects a retrospective
one-increase attempt; ReplayStudio exports a deterministic human- and machine-readable pack.
MarketTwin, ExecutionLab, and CapitalAllocator are absent because no plant network, position,
order, execution, portfolio, allocation, or return is represented.

```bash
  --input-lock scenarios/fed-g17-2020/input-lock.json \

  --input-lock scenarios/fed-g17-2020/input-lock.json \
  --event-lock scenarios/fed-g17-2020/event-lock.json \
  --pack verification/replaypacks/fed-g17-2020 \
  --receipt verification/evidence/fed-g17-four-engine-rebuild.json

```
