# Scenario explorer

This file is generated by `python scripts/build_public_replaypack_downloads.py --write`
from the validated package resource `src/finreplay/resources/scenario-explorer.json`.
It complements the capability catalog rather than replacing it:

- capability scope says whether support is direct, transferable, or boundary-only;
- analytical dimensions describe what a case touches; and
- pathways provide a cross-case reading order without creating a new result.

## Analytical dimensions

### Point-in-time integrity

**Question:** What was actually knowable at the decision boundary?

Availability time, economic time, revisions, and later disclosures remain separate instead of being collapsed into hindsight.

### Revision and range discipline

**Question:** Does the conclusion survive vintage changes and an explicitly uncalibrated range?

Initial releases, revisions, arithmetic envelopes, and sampling limits stay visible; a heuristic range is never relabelled as a forecast or confidence interval.

### Stress and uncertainty

**Question:** Where does the predeclared boundary hold, breach, or remain unevaluable?

Outcomes preserve breaches, inside-range results, reversals, and failed promotion instead of presenting only favorable cases.

### Financial systems and markets

**Question:** How do funding, rates, liquidity, market structure, and balance-sheet constraints interact?

Cases cover bank funding, policy rates, cash balances, repo conditions, auctions, yield curves, credit, and market positioning with explicit claim limits.

### Allocation and execution

**Question:** What survives non-zero cost, capacity, and constrained allocation?

Only the seven-engine bank replays carry simulated execution and allocation artifacts; those values remain research boundaries, not performance.

### Data systems and governance

**Question:** Can another person trace the inputs, transformations, claims, and exclusions?

Typed artifacts, input locks, deterministic archives, hashes, evidence classes, and negative claim boundaries make the workflow auditable.

### Policy and population signals

**Question:** What can an aggregate official indicator support without becoming a causal policy claim?

Labor, inflation, fiscal, monetary, trade, and facility data are treated as population-level or system-level signals, not individual outcomes or policy-effectiveness proof.

### Households and behavior

**Question:** What do aggregate spending, saving, credit, and housing signals reveal—and what motives remain unobserved?

Household-facing indicators support bounded behavioral hypotheses while preserving the gap between aggregate movement and individual intent.

### Housing, construction, and place

**Question:** How do national housing and construction indicators change a place-based question without answering it?

Housing starts, construction spending, house prices, and new-home sales expose spatially relevant evidence while stopping short of local regeneration or equity claims.

### Real-economy operations

**Question:** How do production, inventory, trade, and price shocks move through an operating environment?

Energy stocks, production, orders, trade, and producer or cross-border prices provide bounded operational signals without firm-level causal attribution.
