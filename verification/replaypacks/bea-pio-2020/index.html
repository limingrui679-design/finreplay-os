  • This lock preserves the reported personal saving rates for January and February 2020 from paired official archived BEA Personal Income and Outlays HTML/PDF release snapshots knowable before the replay decision time.   • The April 30 release and its February revision are excluded from every decision input.
  • Two release snapshots do not identify household behavior, causes, or distribution.
  • The range has no policy, return, or forecast interpretation.
  • No position, order, portfolio, allocation, or return is represented.
  • A fixed build epoch and zero elapsed time preserve deterministic pack identity.
