Pack limitations

  • This lock preserves the final SOFR rates effective September 13 and 16, 2019 from two official New York Fed historical API responses conservatively final before the replay decision time.   • The endpoints are the minimum and maximum of two known final rates only.
  • The September 17 rate is excluded from every decision input and artifact.
  • The 3:00 p.m. finality time is conservative rather than an exact release instant.
  • Two aggregate rates do not identify trades, counterparties, flows, or causes.
  • No position, order, portfolio, allocation, or return is represented.
  • A fixed build epoch and zero elapsed time preserve deterministic pack identity.
