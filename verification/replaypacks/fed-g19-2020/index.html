Federal Reserve G.19 boundary before the March 2020 revolving-credit contraction

Four actual engines ran over two locked G.19 revolving-credit monthly changes in the April 7 release snapshot and available at the decision time. January's revised -270-basis-point value and February's preliminary 460-basis-point value remain reported evidence. The 460-to-1,190-basis-point persistence-or-repeat-known-increase range remains inferred with no probability. TrialCourt rejects retrospective promotion. The May 7 March value and later January/February revisions are held only in a disjoint post-decision event lock.
